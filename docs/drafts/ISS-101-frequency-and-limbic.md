## 1. The frequency axis of the field (for the Zoomable Somatic Field paper, Scale 6)

Where: after the Scale 6 equation parameters, which already take the gamma band for the brain's
wavenumber ($k \approx 2\pi \times 40\,\text{Hz} / 6\,\text{m s}^{-1} \approx 40\,\text{m}^{-1}$).

Draft text:

> **The frequency axis.** The wavenumber above uses one frequency, the gamma band's 40 Hz. The
> brain's field is not one frequency but a spectrum of rhythms, conventionally divided into bands:
> delta (0.5-4 Hz), theta (4-8 Hz), alpha (8-13 Hz), beta (13-30 Hz) and gamma (30-100 Hz)
> [@buzsaki2004neuronal] `sourced`. The same equation holds in each band with its own
> $k = \omega / c_\text{neural}$, so the brain level is a family of scales in frequency, as the
> zoom is a family in length `derived-under-assumptions` (the same dispersion, assumed across the
> bands). Coherent oscillation is a proposed mechanism by which neural groups communicate
> [@fries2005mechanism] `sourced` (as a proposal in the literature). Which of the model's types a
> band carries is not derived here; one testable reading is offered below.

> **A hypothesis: the hinge as cross-frequency coupling.** If the limbic hinge (D8) and the
> cortical mind field (D9-11) are carried by different bands, theta for the hippocampal-limbic
> rhythm and gamma for cortical binding, then the hinge between them is theta-gamma coupling, in
> which the phase of a slow rhythm modulates the amplitude of a fast one
> [@canolty2006high; @lisman2013theta] `sourced` (that the coupling exists). That the model's D8 to
> D9-11 correspondence *is* this coupling is `open-hypothesis`: it predicts that an emotion becoming
> a thought (the model's two readings of one event) shows as stronger theta-gamma coupling between
> limbic and cortical sites at that moment.

New references:

- Buzsáki, G., & Draguhn, A. (2004). Neuronal oscillations in cortical networks. *Science*,
  304(5679), 1926-1929. (key `buzsaki2004neuronal`)
- Canolty, R. T., et al. (2006). High gamma power is phase-locked to theta oscillations in human
  neocortex. *Science*, 313(5793), 1626-1628. (key `canolty2006high`)
- Lisman, J. E., & Jensen, O. (2013). The theta-gamma neural code. *Neuron*, 77(6), 1002-1016.
  (key `lisman2013theta`)
- Fries (2005) is already in the bibliography (`fries2005mechanism`).

**The question for the author:** is the theta-gamma reading the one you want in the paper, or
should the paper state the frequency axis only (the first paragraph) and leave the mapping as a
named gap?

## 2. The limbic route (for P14, The Missing Limbic Layer)

Where: P14 places the limbic hinge at $D_8$, between the body field ($D_{1-7}$) and the cortical
mind field ($D_{9-11}$), but does not say which anatomy carries it.

Draft text:

> **The route through the hinge.** In the brain the limbic structures form a loop around the
> ventricles: the hippocampus, which forms the floor of the lateral ventricle's temporal horn,
> projects through the fornix, which arches over the thalamus, to the mammillary bodies of the
> hypothalamus; from there the mammillothalamic tract runs to the anterior thalamus, which
> projects to the cingulate gyrus above the corpus callosum, and the cingulum returns to the
> hippocampal region: the circuit Papez proposed as the substrate of emotion [@papez1937proposed]
> `sourced` (the anatomy; Papez's functional claim is historical, and the hippocampus is now known
> chiefly for memory). The amygdala, just in front of the temporal horn's tip, is the structure
> later work placed at the centre of fear and threat [@ledoux1996emotional] `sourced`. We take
> this loop, with the amygdala, as the anatomical carrier of the $D_8$ hinge `interpretive`: the
> place where the body field's signals (through the hypothalamus) and the cortical field (through
> the cingulate) meet.

New reference:

- Papez, J. W. (1937). A proposed mechanism of emotion. *Archives of Neurology and Psychiatry*,
  38(4), 725-743. (key `papez1937proposed`)
- LeDoux (1996) is already in the bibliography (`ledoux1996emotional`).

**The question for the author:** the app's LIMBIC road follows exactly this loop from the
cathedral's temporal horn; is the Papez loop with the amygdala the anatomy you want P14 to name,
or should P14 stay with the amygdala and the hippocampus only?

Already written as a model gap in the app's design (`apps/instrument/visuals/soma-field-operator/
DESIGN.md`, "A model gap: the inner voice"): inner speech in the language network; the author's
hypothesis that it becomes conscious by joining the brain's field (CEMI, classical, not quantum).
