This is the script for the thirty-minute film *Be the size*: a tour of the Soma Machine, zoom first, with your
voice over the flight. It is made from the scenes (`registry/scenes.yaml`); change the words there, and this
script and the film follow.

**How to read the page.**

- The **words** are in large type, one breath to a line. Read a line, breathe, read the next.
- A grey stroke **/** is a short pause; **//** a longer one. They are suggestions: your own phrasing wins.
- An underlined word is the one to lean on.
- **HOW** says how to say it, in plain words. You are not acting: you are telling a friend.
- The small box above (**SCREEN**, **MUSIC**) says what is happening around you, so you know where you are.
  The music is composed around your voice: it thins while you speak and swells when you stop. You do not need to
  leave gaps for it.
- **THE HOOK** is a separate take: the scene's question and its answer, spoken, then half-sung, then sung.
- Record each scene as its own take, named by the scene (`record.ps1 --scene <name>` shows you the words). A
  fluff is fine: say the line again, and carry on.

The facts in the words for U follow OpenStax (*Astronomy 2e*, *Biology 2e*, *Psychology 2e*; CC BY 4.0) and the
app's own level files; each scene names its source.
