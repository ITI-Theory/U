The regional level tracks bounded propagation through geography and institutions. The civilisational-solar level asks what persists when many regions are coupled for long periods. Local jurisdictions, towns, and corridors are integrated out into larger attractors: energy regimes, archives, trade networks, imperial or federal structures, or, on the physical branch, orbital basins. `interpretive`: What survives is response grammar and path dependence; what changes is the state space. `open-hypothesis`: The hybrid level is not a proof that societies and solar systems share a substance. It needs new variables because long-horizon stability depends on records, resource flows, gravitational wells, or basin structure, not merely on regional diffusion speed.
