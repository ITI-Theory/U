Zooming from a galactic disc to the observable universe replaces one rotating baryonic system with a causal survey volume. Some structures survive: gravity, density contrast, rotation histories, feedback products, and dark-halo inference all contribute to the large-scale matter map `interpretive`. What is averaged away is almost everything local to a galaxy: spiral-arm microstructure, star-forming clouds, bars, and individual stellar populations become statistical tracers or bias parameters `derived-under-assumptions`. The upper level needs redshift, horizon, expansion history, power spectrum, and density parameters because observations arrive on the past light cone, not from a simultaneous map. Cosmological [T]-Theory ratios such as $7/11$ and $3/11$ are model-dependent comparisons beside Planck 2018, not measurements `derived-under-assumptions`.
