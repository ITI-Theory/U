At geological scale the focus is a fault, fold, basin, or rock volume: stress is stored locally and released through deformation or seismic waves. At planetary scale those details become part of a coupled heat and momentum budget. `empirical-result`: mantle convection, plate motion, ocean circulation, atmospheric waves, and magnetic response are measured geophysical processes. `interpretive`: The Atlas keeps the impulse-response grammar but averages individual structures into boundary conditions, heat flux, viscosity, rotation, gravity, and circulation. The upper level needs new variables because a planet is not just more rock; it is a nested solid-fluid-electromagnetic system with feedbacks across interior, surface, atmosphere, ocean, and space environment.
