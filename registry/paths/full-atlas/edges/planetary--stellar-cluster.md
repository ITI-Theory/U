Zooming from the planetary level to the stellar-cluster level changes the object of study from one coupled planetary system to a population of luminous bodies and remnants moving in a shared many-body field. Orbital history survives: angular momentum, resonance, capture, ejection, and tidal stripping remain legible in phase space `interpretive`. What is averaged away is planetary surface detail: atmosphere, geology, oceans, and local chemistry no longer drive the displayed variables except through mass, luminosity effects, or debris signatures `derived-under-assumptions`. The upper level needs new variables because pairwise orbits do not describe relaxation, mass segregation, tidal tails, binaries, and cluster evaporation. The [T]-Theory reading is response grammar only, not a claim that clusters or planets have subjective states `interpretive`.
