This is an honest transition at the same nominal scale, not a claim that the universe becomes larger. Both levels sit near $10^{26}$ m. The observable-universe plate names the causally accessible horizon volume: the domain from which light, gravitational waves, neutrinos, and other signals can reach the observer under a cosmological model `empirical-result`. The cosmic-web plate names the large-scale structure inside that domain: nodes, filaments, sheets, voids, BAO features, tracer bias, and survey topology `empirical-result`.

What survives is causal boundary discipline. Past-light-cone geometry, redshift, expansion history, selection functions, and Planck-era parameter fits still constrain the display. What is integrated out is the totalising view of the whole horizon as one object. The upper display re-indexes the same volume by connectivity and topology, so individual catalogues, probes, and shells become layers in a web map `derived-under-assumptions`.

`interpretive`: The Atlas reads the step as a change from volume boundary to structure grammar. The response kernel becomes explicitly retarded: only signals inside the light cone can be part of the rendered web. The transition adds no cosmic subject and proves no universal consciousness `open-hypothesis`. It simply distinguishes two legitimate views at one scale: the whole observable domain versus the patterned matter distribution that surveys and simulations reconstruct within it.
