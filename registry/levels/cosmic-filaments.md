## In ordinary science

Cosmic filaments are elongated bridges of dark matter, galaxies, gas, and diffuse baryons connecting clusters and groups. They are part of the large-scale structure that emerged as tiny early density fluctuations grew by gravity. Filaments are not solid tubes; they are statistical overdensities in a web of walls, nodes, and voids. Surveys identify them from galaxy positions, weak lensing, X-ray and Sunyaev-Zel'dovich signals, and simulations [@bond1996cosmicweb; @tempel2014filaments].

The registry equation $(\partial_\eta^2+2\mathcal H\partial_\eta-c_s^2\nabla^2)G_{18}=\delta^{(4)}$ is a cosmological perturbation response. In words, a density impulse evolves in an expanding universe; expansion damps growth, effective pressure or sound speed resists collapse on some scales, and gravity amplifies overdensities. The equation is schematic for the atlas, because real filament formation requires nonlinear gravity, gas heating, feedback, and survey selection effects.

Lengths are tens to hundreds of megaparsecs. Timescales are cosmic: filaments trace billions of years of accretion and merger history. They channel galaxies and gas toward nodes, align spins statistically, and host much of the warm-hot intergalactic medium. Filaments require variables that clusters do not: skeleton topology, connectivity, linear density, cross-section, node degree, void boundary, and environmental anisotropy.

Filaments are measured through ensembles. A single filament can be ambiguous, but repeated maps across surveys and simulations make the web-like pattern a robust feature of structure formation [@springel2005millennium].

The physical contents are mixed. Dark matter supplies most of the gravitating scaffold, galaxies mark high-density knots, and hot or warm-hot gas may hold a significant fraction of ordinary baryons. Because the signal is faint, filament claims are strongest when geometry, lensing, galaxy density, and gas measurements agree across a statistically defined sample.

Filaments therefore sit between object astronomy and field statistics: their boundaries are defined by algorithms as much as by images.

Their physical reality is tested by repeatable correlations across tracers.

## The [T]-Theory reading

The programme reads a filament as a preserved channel of cosmological response: early perturbations and later gravitational flows become a connected matter skeleton `interpretive` [@johnson2026usf]. It carries up clusters, halos, galaxies, and gas histories, while internal galaxy details are integrated out into density, connectivity, and flow variables `derived-under-assumptions`.

This level is close to the cosmology papers, but the [T]-Theory claim remains model-bound. *The Cosmological Constant as the Vacuum Amplitude of the Universal Somatic Field* and *Dark Matter as the Spatial Vacuum of the Universal Somatic Field: Ω_DM = 3/11* offer comparisons under assumptions, not independent measurements `derived-under-assumptions` [@johnson2026cosconst; @johnson2026darkmatter]. No cognition, intention, or subjective state is attributed to filaments.

The atlas use of connectivity is therefore topological and dynamical, not psychological `interpretive`.
