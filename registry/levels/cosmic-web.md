## In ordinary science

The cosmic web is the largest mapped pattern of matter: clusters at nodes, filaments as bridges, sheets as walls, and underdense voids between them. It is the nonlinear descendant of early-universe perturbations seen statistically in the cosmic microwave background and later in galaxy surveys. Gravity turns initially small density contrasts into a web whose topology depends on dark matter, baryons, expansion history, and initial conditions [@peebles1980large; @springel2005millennium].

The registry equation $G_{20}(x,x')=\theta(t-t')\delta((x-x')^2)/(2\pi)$ is a retarded light-cone propagator. The step function $\theta(t-t')$ enforces that the response comes after the source; the delta term localises propagation on the light cone in the simplified expression. In cosmology, real propagation is shaped by expansion, curvature, gravitational potentials, and observation through our past light cone.

The cosmic web spans hundreds to thousands of megaparsecs. Measurements include redshift surveys, baryon acoustic oscillation scales, weak lensing, cluster counts, CMB anisotropy, and simulations. BAO features provide a standard ruler left by early acoustic waves in the photon-baryon plasma [@eisenstein2005bao]. Planck 2018 gives a flat $\Lambda$CDM baseline with $H_0=67.36$ km s$^{-1}$ Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Omega_\Lambda=0.6847$ [@planck2018cosmology].

This level needs horizon, selection-function, and survey-volume variables. It is not merely a larger galaxy map; it is a causal and statistical reconstruction from finite, light-travel-time-limited data.

The web is also model-tested through correlation functions and power spectra. A survey does not list structure neutrally: it has magnitude limits, masks, redshift errors, and tracer bias. Simulations connect those imperfect catalogues to gravitational growth, while the CMB fixes early conditions against which late-time structure is compared.

For this reason the web is both a map and a statistical object with uncertainties attached.

It is drawn from catalogues, but validated through reproducible clustering measures, not through visual resemblance alone.
