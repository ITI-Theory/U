# [T]-Theory: Philosophy

Source for the philosophy volume of the Fractal Thesis (`book-philosophy` in
`../books.yaml`): `book-philosophy.md` and `booklet-philosophy.md`. The build
is described in `../README.md`.

## Decisions (author, 2026-10-01)

1. The six-part shape stands: the object, Russell's method, the
   time-invariant history, knowledge/order/proof, the hard problem revisited,
   wrap-up.
2. Part III follows Russell's divisions in *A History of Western Philosophy*;
   Part IV carries his method on past 1945.
3. Chapter 2 summarises how the programme was made; *Phase Dot* is the source
   record and is still incomplete.
4. Length: 80,000–95,000 words.
5. The title is *[T]-Theory: Philosophy*; "Dissolved" was dropped from the
   earlier working title.

Pribram and McFadden's CEMI sit in Chapter 15, beside the other neighbouring
theories, not in Chapter 10.

The original brief and outline are kept for history in
`docs/agent/PHILOSOPHY-BOOK-BRIEF.md.OBSOLETE` and
`docs/agent/PHILOSOPHY-BOOK-OUTLINE.md.OBSOLETE`.
