# [T]-Theory: A Complete Programme

## Science, Art, and the Universal Field

**[T]-Theory** is both a scientific framework and an art movement. The brackets around the T are intentional: they mark the boundary between the formal system (the mathematics inside) and the cultural practice (the art, music, and lived philosophy that surrounds it).

Inside the brackets is **Soma Field Theory (SFT)** — a proposed mathematical account of consciousness, emotion, and physical reality as manifestations of a single tensor-valued field, organised through M-theory compactification assumptions and related formal models. The science is not presented here as finished confirmation. Lean 4 checks named formal statements under the definitions and axioms in the repository: `FieldAxioms.lean` is an axiom registry with twenty axioms, and the present proof surface still contains five real `sorry`s. Where a named theorem compiles, it is `kernel-verified` only as a formal statement about its definitions and assumptions. QUANT-EXP-1 is an exact eight-qubit, 256-state statevector simulation, not a hardware quantum experiment. The cosmological constant and dark-matter numbers are model-derived comparisons, `derived-under-assumptions`, not independent empirical confirmation. Clinical, trauma, and lived-experience material remain testimony and `open-hypothesis`, not claims of clinical efficacy.

Outside the brackets is something harder to name and easier to feel: an underground rave aesthetic, a street art collective, a philosophy of life that takes seriously the possibility that the universe is not merely described by equations but *is* an experience — that the field is not a representation of reality but reality itself, experienced from the inside.

These two things — the science and the art — are not in tension. They are the same investigation conducted with different instruments. The physicist uses Lean 4, model equations, and simulations. The DJ uses a Strandberg guitar and an Ableton Push. Both are asking the same question: what does the somatic field do, and how do we work with it?

Each book stands alone. A geophysicist can read book 8 without reading any of the others. A trauma therapist can read book 13. A music psychologist can start at book 7.

But the books also form a system. A shared formal grammar and recurring equation schema move through the books — reinterpreted for each domain, written in each domain's vocabulary, and marked where the move is analogy, mathematical co-identification, or claimed identity. The physicist's Green's function, the sociologist's rapport propagator, the lawyer's rights invariant, and the therapist's trauma well are not asserted to be interchangeable empirical objects; they are coordinated readings of a common formal programme, some `interpretive`, some `derived-under-assumptions`, and some still open to failure.

In the print edition, each canonical paper is printed once per print volume. Where another book needs the same paper, it receives a cross-reference rather than a second full printing. The prose around those cross-references is therefore part of the apparatus: it tells the reader why the paper matters here, even when the paper itself appears elsewhere in the physical volume.

## How to Navigate This Volume

If you have no specialist background: start with Book 1 (Gateway). It requires nothing except curiosity.

If you are a scientist: start with the book for your field. The kappa — the domain-specific introduction — is written for you. The papers that follow are the primary literature.

If you want the mathematics first: start with Books 2 (Physics) or 3 (Mathematics). These are the most technically demanding and the most foundational.

If you have been handed this by someone who works in the field and wanted you to understand what they are doing: Book 1 was written for exactly that situation.

If you want the retrospective question — what consciousness, effect, formal proof, art practice, and the author's philosophical wager look like after the technical programme has been unfolded — read Book 15, **[T]-Theory: Philosophy**. It looks back across the system rather than trying to replace the specialist volumes.

If you are loading this into a research AI or language model: the structure is intentional. Each book is self-contained as a reading path. The cross-references between books mark formal, interpretive, and hypothetical connections rather than a claim that every domain mapping has the same evidential status. The kappas are the entry points; the papers are the depth.
