# [T]-Theory: A Complete Programme

## Science, Art, and the Universal Field

**[T]-Theory** is both a scientific framework and an art movement. The brackets around the T are intentional: they mark the boundary between the formal system (the mathematics inside) and the cultural practice (the art, music, and lived philosophy that surrounds it).

Inside the brackets is **Soma Field Theory (SFT)** — a mathematical account of consciousness, emotion, and physical reality as manifestations of a single tensor-valued field derived from M-theory compactification. The science is rigorous, formally verified in Lean 4, and experimentally tested. 22 papers have been published on Zenodo. The derivations are complete. The proofs are machine-checked. The cosmological constant and dark matter density are both derived to within 7% from pure dimensional counting.

Outside the brackets is something harder to name and easier to feel: an underground rave aesthetic, a street art collective, a philosophy of life that takes seriously the possibility that the universe is not merely described by equations but *is* an experience — that the field is not a representation of reality but reality itself, experienced from the inside.

These two things — the science and the art — are not in tension. They are the same investigation conducted with different instruments. The physicist uses Lean 4 and a D-Wave quantum annealer. The DJ uses a Strandberg guitar and an Ableton push. Both are asking the same question: what does the somatic field do, and how do we work with it?

## How to Navigate This Volume

If you have no specialist background: start with Book 1 (Gateway). It requires nothing except curiosity.

If you are a scientist: start with the book for your field. The kappa — the domain-specific introduction — is written for you. The papers that follow are the primary literature.

If you want the mathematics first: start with Books 2 (Physics) or 7 (Formal Mathematics). These are the most technically demanding and the most foundational.

If you have been handed this by someone who works in the field and wanted you to understand what they are doing: Book 1 was written for exactly that situation.

If you are loading this into a research AI or language model: the structure is intentional. Each book is self-contained and internally consistent. The cross-references between books reflect genuine mathematical connections. The kappas are the entry points; the papers are the depth.
