# [T]-Theory

Soma Field Theory and related work: a research programme that proposes one field, read at
every scale from the cosmos to a single thought. Papers, books, a Field Atlas, Lean proofs and
an app. Every claim carries its label: proved in Lean, derived under stated assumptions,
interpretive, or speculative.

## Ask the theory first

Before the app, ask the theory itself:
[MOTHER](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/16368cb3-6c5f-47b3-8e79-781b77084944), a
NotebookLM notebook that holds the papers and books and answers with its sources (a Google
account is needed). If she answers you, you are set up for the rest.

## Two ways in

**For anyone.** [The Soma Machine](https://www.t-theory.org/app/): a mind-and-body explorer, from the whole body down
to a thought (a desktop browser on a large screen works best). It opens with a choice: take the
blue button.

**For the scientist.** The papers and books with their DOIs on
[Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/iti-theory/records); the sources, the Lean proofs and
the build on [GitHub](https://github.com/ITI-Theory). In the app, take the red button.

*The app is a preview: in user acceptance testing, not a release.*
