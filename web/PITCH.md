# The pitch, and where it goes

Copilot's proposal, 16 Oct 2026 (ISS-109, ISS-111, ISS-112); the author decides. Counts are not
copied here: they live in `Dist/PAPERS.yaml`, `bin/release-check` and Sherlock's reports.

## For anyone (U)

The Soma Machine is a mind-and-body explorer. It helps you think about your body and the
sensations in it, from your feet to your head, and about your mind: its moods, its thoughts,
the paths it takes. Wear a heart-rate band and the picture follows your heart. Explore by
looking, as you would in an art gallery, an abstract film or a book; or take the guided tour of
a mind palace. It is only a guided tour: the real tour is your own, as in a guided meditation.

A teaching picture of a model, not medical advice and not a measurement of your mind.

## For the scientist

[T]-Theory proposes one field, the Soma Field, read at every scale from cosmology to a single
thought, set out in papers and books with Lean 4 proofs. Every claim carries its label:
kernel-verified, derived under stated assumptions, interpretive, or speculative
(`docs/agent/THEORY-STATUS.md`). Sherlock checks that no text claims more than its proof, and
that no book promises more than it covers. The app draws the model with the same labels; the
notebook answers from the sources. Start with the papers on Zenodo; the build is on GitHub.

## A new universe (the author's, 16 Oct; humour)

"I have been analysing myself, to find the real underlying issue. I've had it with people
blaming others: if it's me, fine, let's get to the very core. And it turns out, after years of
therapy, yes, I am part of the problem. That's fine, because I can be part of the solution.
And yes, the problem is the whole Universe, and I am indeed a part of that. Once you know what
the problem is, you can start the work. But not a shoot-them-up, blame-the-others approach. No:
if this universe is not up to it, let's fix it by creating a new one. Here's what I had in
mind."

Then the app: a score becomes a universe, a galaxy with its stars and planets that SpaceEngine
can fly through (`lib/universe/universe.py`, ISS-112). A picture of a score, not cosmology:
`speculative`, a show.
