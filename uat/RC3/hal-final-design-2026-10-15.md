# HAL-UAT: the Soma Machine, the final design (15 Oct 2026, asked by Copilot for the author)

The author asked Copilot to hold this conversation for him and to aim at the final version of
what the app should be: the app, not the printed books, is the programme's show piece, so it has
to look good. Each turn builds on the last.

## Q1. The inside of a mind

What the Soma Machine is, as the author put it on 15 Oct: an instrument, not a simulator; the user
is the machine. The elevator is a physics model (one field at 21 scales, quantum foam to cosmic
web); the body and brain are biology, educational, entered from a 3D humanoid (the jellyfish
human) by diving into start places. Each start place is an atrium, a chamber seen from inside
(built: the brain's great hall, the atrium of the lateral ventricle under water, neon veins on
dark stone, the choroid plexus as a chandelier, the arachnoid's webs, jellyfish; the heart's
left atrium with the mitral valve beating; the neuron's soma with its nucleus), with tubes
leaving it (arteries, ventricles, nerves): two shapes, the chamber and the tube. Step 1: explore
them as physical places only.

Then the mind, in the author's words of 15 Oct: "I'm hearing words in my mind, I'm seeing pictures
in my mind, and occasionally emotions and pain, flashbacks. It's sort of black and they pop in.
Whereas you have a tube with an emotion flowing, I think that's wrong; it's more like the quantum
foam where they're there bubbling and they occasionally pop into reality in my head; when they
spark it becomes a sphere: a chamber rather than a river, because they're not there, then they're
there, then they flow away. With complex PTSD a thought doesn't flow away, it stays: it rises up
a little and stays. So: the foam; the thought; the thoughts that stay (twelve, thirty of them).
And paths: I want to go skiing, what have I got to do? This way, that way, a third way: each a
path. Bubbles on one hand, paths on the other: this needs sorting."

The model (Lean): a feeling is a blend of eight basic emotions evoked by one of eight mechanisms
(EmotionOntology); below a threshold the field is real but not felt, above it conscious (the
consciousness dichotomy); one event is an emotion at D8 and a thought at D9-11 (FieldEvent);
memories and stuck states are attractors, Φ heats the landscape, C-PTSD raises the barriers
(LimbicHopfield, HopfieldAsync); leaving a well is tunnelling (LimbicTunnel); the past acts
through a memory kernel (TemporalDynamics); transitions depend on the route (the path-sensitive
transitions paper). The app's pictures are checked against these terms; not yet shown: the
blend, the mechanism, the route.

From the author's chats and notes: does the picture "a dark chamber; foam below; thoughts that
spark into spheres of their kind (words, a picture, a feeling, a pain, a flashback) and drift
away; the ones that stay, rising and holding; paths leaving the chamber for a plan's options"
fit what he has said before (name the chat and date)? What is missing? And what should each
model quantity look like in it: the blend, the mechanism, the threshold, the band, the depth of
a well, the barrier, the temperature, the route? Keep to what the model says; mark anything
interpretive as such.

## Q2. One machine, not two apps

The author's first impressions on 15 Oct: "awesome", but navigation is the issue: a mix of static
views, moving ones, diagrams and fractals; the levels view (the first app: the zoom dial, the
level panels, the console) has a different feel from the mind-body explorer, "it looks like two
apps"; in the explorer he is not sure how the buttons fit together ("it's all there, it's just
presentation"). The explorer has fourteen stops in a row (human, body, way in, brain, limbic,
thought, neuron, memory, landscape, dyad, path, ureter, fly, score) and a second row of about
twenty toggles and sliders. There is also an overview of the desk (one screen and three tablets),
a cockpit view, an engine room, the elevator with STEP OUT, and the atriums with doorways.

From the chats: how did the author describe moving through the machine (the elevator, the
cockpit, the voyage, stepping out, the desk, the Fantastic Voyage)? Propose one structure for
the whole app in which every view has its place: what is a place, what is a view of a place,
what is a control, what is a setting; and which of today's buttons go where. Keep it simple
enough to grasp at a glance.

## Q3. The look: what the author has considered

From the chats and notes, list the images, films, artists and references the author has
considered for the look of the inside (and of the whole app), with the chat and date: for
example Fantastic Voyage (1966), Hackers, Tron, Kandinsky, neon blue and purple, an underwater
cathedral, jellyfish, HoloGauze and Pepper's ghost, the alpine barn, the Mandelbulb. Which of
them fit the atriums' standard (dark stone, thin neon veins pink above and blue below, light
from above, the level's own anatomy, drifters, glowing named doorways), and which should be
dropped or kept for ROOTED (the unlocked, non-science mode)?

## Q4. The final version

Bring Q1 to Q3 together as the final design of the Soma Machine, in one page: the places and the
mind chamber; what the user sees first; how one moves; what each control does; the pictures of
the model's terms; the look. Then the order to build it in (what first, what last, what to leave
out), so the app can stand as the programme's show piece.
