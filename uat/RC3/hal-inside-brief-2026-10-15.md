# For HAL: how should the inside of a mind be shown? (15 Oct 2026; Copilot's summary, for the author to paste and add his take)

**What the Soma Machine is.** An instrument, not a simulator: the user is the machine. Two
parts that are physical and educational: the elevator, a physics model of one field at 21
scales from the quantum foam to the cosmic web; and the body and brain, biology, entered from
a 3D humanoid (the jellyfish human) by diving into start places. The start places are atriums:
a chamber seen from inside (the brain's great hall, the atrium of the lateral ventricle under
water; the heart's left atrium; the neuron's cell body, the soma), with tubes leaving each
(arteries, ventricles, nerves: rivers, tunnels, wormholes). Two shapes are enough: the
chamber and the tube. Step 1 is to explore these as physical places only, with no mind in
them.

**Then the mind.** The question is what a thought, a feeling, a pain or a flashback should look
like there. Forward, from experience: words heard in the mind, pictures seen in it, now and
then an emotion, a pain, a flashback; the background is dark, and they pop in. Not a tube
with an emotion flowing along it: more like the quantum foam, bubbling under the surface, and
now and then one sparks into reality as a sphere, then drifts away. So the inside of the mind
is a chamber, not a river. Three levels: (1) the foam, below awareness; (2) the thought that
pops, of a kind (words, a picture, a feeling, a pain); (3) the thought that stays: with
complex PTSD a thought does not drift away but rises a little and stays, and there can be
twelve or thirty of them staying. And then paths: "I want to go skiing; what must I get done?
I could do it this way, or that way, or a third way": each option is a path. So the mind has
both: bubbles in a chamber and paths leaving it.

**Backward, from the model (the Lean).** What the programme's own model says can be expressed,
so that a picture is checked against it (a thought drawn as a bar of chocolate would express
none of it, and Sherlock should say so):
- A feeling is a combination: eight basic emotions blended or damped, evoked by one of eight
  mechanisms (brain stem, conditioning, contagion, visual imagery, episodic memory, rhythmic
  entrainment, musical expectancy, aesthetic judgement) (EmotionOntology.lean; the field over
  those eight, SomaField.lean).
- Below a threshold the field is real but not felt (preconscious); above it, conscious
  (UniversalSomaticField.lean, the consciousness dichotomy): the foam and the pop.
- One field, different types: the same state is an emotion in the limbic band (D8) and a
  thought in the cortical band (D9 to D11) (FieldEvent.lean): a feeling and a thought are one
  event read at two levels.
- Memories and stuck states are attractors of an energy landscape; the limbic field Φ heats
  the landscape (stress raises the temperature); C-PTSD raises the barriers, so a state cannot
  leave its well; ADHD runs hotter, autism colder (LimbicHopfield.lean, HopfieldAsync.lean):
  the thought that stays.
- Leaving a well is tunnelling through its barrier (LimbicTunnel.lean); the past keeps acting
  through a memory kernel (TemporalDynamics.lean); a poke is seen only after it is made
  (EffectSpec.lean).
- Paths: the transitions between basins depend on the route taken (the path-sensitive
  transitions paper): the options in a plan.
