# HAL-UAT: never written, what the chats discuss that no book covers (ISS-092; 16 Oct 2026)

## Q1. Which ideas from the chats never made it into a book or paper?

From the chats and notes only: list the ideas, claims or stories the author discussed at length
(several conversations, or one long one) that no paper or book of the programme covers. For each:
the chat and date, one line on the idea, and why it matters (a gap in an argument, a story a book
would want, a promise made and not kept). Leave out what the books already say in other words,
and leave out software and admin. At most ten, the strongest first.

## Q2. Which promises in the chats are still open?

Where did the author say "this goes in the book", "I'll write this up", "this needs a paper", or
plan a chapter or section, and the books or papers do not have it? Quote the promise with the
chat and date, and say which book or paper it was meant for.

## Q3. What would a careful reader miss most?

If a reader had only the books and papers, which part of the author's thinking in the chats would
they miss most: an argument, a caveat, a limitation he named himself, or a story that explains why
the programme exists? Answer in at most five points, each with its chat and date.
