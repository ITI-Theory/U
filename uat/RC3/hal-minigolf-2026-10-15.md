## Q1. Six holes: which basins does the model name?

The Soma Machine's LANDSCAPE stop is to become a six-hole mini golf (author, 15 Oct): each hole a
basin (an attractor) of the field's energy landscape; the ball is the current state; the paths
between holes are transitions, wide where the barrier is low and the crossing frequent (a
fairway), thin where it is high and rare (a wire), a bridge that opens only with the resource
(the forcing J, help or therapy), and a tunnel for barrier tunnelling (open hypothesis); the paths
are asymmetric (easy into FREEZE, hard out). The model already names SAFE, FIGHT and FREEZE (the
landscape's three wells) and AWE (the remote basin of P2 and QUANT-EXP-1, from FEAR). From the
papers, the Lean (LimbicHopfield, EmotionOntology, QuantumSim, LimbicTunnel, SomaField) and the
chats: which two further basins does the model itself name or imply, so that the six are the
model's, not invented (for example a shutdown or collapse state, flow, grief, joy, rumination,
or the Hopfield memories)? For each of the six: where it is named (paper, Lean declaration, chat
and date), what the model says about its depth and the barriers to and from the others (which
crossings are easy, which rare, which need the resource, which only by tunnelling), and its
evidence status (kernel-verified, derived under assumptions, simulated, interpretive, open).
Say plainly where the model says nothing, rather than filling the gap.
