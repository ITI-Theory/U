The author's walkaround of the Soma Machine (ISS-102) turned up a set of ideas about the inside
of the mind; before building them we ask HAL-UAT, the notebook that holds the author's chats,
what he has already said. Each question carries the brief, since HAL-UAT does not hold the app.

The brief. In the app's mind-body explorer the brain's ventricles are drawn as a cathedral (the
CAVE): a fly-through of the fluid-filled cave system (fourth ventricle, aqueduct, third
ventricle, lateral ventricles), thoughts flashing in the stone as neural avalanches, visual

pop only when clicked (go into the thought). The mind palace (21 floors, quantum foam to cosmic
web) now lights in pink and blue when the field Φ is high. A further idea: three elevators, by
scale (exists), by dimension (body D1-7, limbic D8, mind D9-11) and by the brain's EM field
frequency (delta to gamma: a gap in the model).
