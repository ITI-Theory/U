The Wizard's questions (built the right thing) and its adversary's, asked of HAL-UAT, the
notebook that holds the author's chats. HAL-UAT does not hold the release itself, so each
question names what the release contains. Asked with `make ask NB=hal Q="..."`; the answers go
beside this file (hal-uat-answers-rc3.2.md). The cold-reader questions are the tracks' worksheets
(uat/ttheory-nlm-uat.md, uat/papers-omnibus-nlm-uat.md), asked of the UAT notebooks.

explains the same data with fewer assumptions wins.
