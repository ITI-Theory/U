# HAL: the score design round, 18 Oct 2026 (ISS-118)

The musical score before the sound: the original tune, the dots and lines on paper. HAL's sources
include the author's AI Mode music chats (group music-chats).

## Q1 The original tune

Across the author's chats, where does the tune of a piece come from? List every source he has named
(his day as 5-10 tracks in order; a song such as Waiting for the Man chopped into MIDI; free MIDI and
scores; psytrance such as Protonica; Dick Dale, Link Wray's Rumble; his own playing), and for each say
what is kept from it: melody, bass line, rhythm, harmony, or only the feeling.

## Q2 Composer and conductor

The author said: at compose time there is a composer, at play time a conductor, and the user is an
advanced conductor. In his own words from the chats, what does the composer decide, what does the
conductor change while it plays, and what must never change between the two (the emotions underneath)?

## Q3 Emotions to music

How do the author's chats and papers tie the emotions over time (the 32 emotions with values, the
film's emotional score) to musical choices: key and mode, tempo, the build-up, peak, drop and walk-off,
counterpoint, orchestration? Quote the mappings he proposed or accepted.

## Q4 Morphing between scales

The author's model zooms by powers of ten (about 20 scales, from quantum foam to the universe), with
music per scale (foam: Dick Dale's surf drip and spring reverb; the oscillator: Link Wray's Rumble; the
universe: the orchestra; foam: Softube Modular). What did he say about morphing at compose time from one
scale's music to the next, and what makes it the same piece throughout (the fractal idea)?

## Q5 The written score

What has the author said about notation itself: Dorico, Sibelius and its plugins, Cubase, MusicXML,
MIDI files, the piano roll, and the zoomed-out view like Traktor's rolling waveform (a bass line and two
melodies as rolling lines)? What should the master copy of the score be?

## Q6 Outputs and choice

The author owns Traktor 4 and likes its remix slots as a way for a user to pick the final instruments,
and wants more than one output: one plays while the next is worked out. What in his chats supports or
constrains this (196 players, Serum 2 and Phase Plant instances, sub-mixes and stems, hot-swap)?
