### Example: vlogs interview video using drone

Using a drone in vlog or interview videos can add establishing shots,
contextual B-roll, and tracking shots without a camera crew. Useful starting
techniques include high-angle reveals, orbiting location shots, and autonomous
subject tracking. For a cinematic baseline, use a shutter speed approximately
twice the frame rate and an ND filter in bright conditions.
