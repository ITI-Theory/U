## PHYSICAL

At the Planck scale, $10^{-35}$ metres, spacetime itself loses meaning
as a smooth continuum. What we call "space" and "time" are themselves
emergent: they arise from underlying degrees of freedom that, at this
scale, are not yet geometric.

The physical substrate is **discrete spacetime nodes** — the minimal
units from which continuous geometry emerges. There is no well-defined
notion of "distance" or "direction" at this scale. The geometry
fluctuates with amplitude $\delta g \sim 1$ (in Planck units): the
metric is as uncertain as any quantum observable.

What exists at Scale 0:
- The vacuum energy density: $\rho_\text{vac} \sim m_P^4 / \ell_P^3 \approx 10^{113}$ J/m³
- Virtual particle-antiparticle pairs appearing and annihilating
- Quantum fluctuations of the gravitational field with amplitudes of order 1 Planck length

Nothing at this scale has what we would recognise as a stable identity.
Every configuration is equally real. Every history is simultaneously
in play. This is the deepest known physical substrate.

## FIELD

At Scale 0, the propagator $G$ is the **gravitational quantum amplitude**:
the probability amplitude for a graviton (a quantum of gravitational
field) to propagate from spacetime point $x'$ to $x$.

In the Planck-length limit:
$$G_P(x, x') = \langle x | \hat{G} | x' \rangle$$

This is not a classical wave. It is a sum over all paths connecting
$x'$ to $x$, weighted by $e^{iS/\hbar}$ where $S$ is the
gravitational action along each path (the Feynman path integral).

The Green's function satisfies, in the appropriate semiclassical limit:
$$(\nabla^2 + k_P^2)\, G_P(x, x') = \delta^{(4)}(x - x')$$

with $k_P = 1/\ell_P$.

This is the master equation. Already, at Scale 0, in its most
fundamental form.

The "poke" at this scale is a **graviton source** — a quantum of
energy that curves spacetime. The "response" is the subsequent
curvature that propagates away. This is exactly the impulse response
we will see at Scale 10 (seismic waves) and Scale 20 (gravitational
waves from merging black holes). The scale changes. The equation
does not.

## MIND

At Scale 0, the mind matrix has rank $N \to \infty$: all configurations
exist simultaneously in quantum superposition.

This is not metaphor. In quantum mechanics, a system before measurement
is genuinely in all states simultaneously. The "mind" at Scale 0 is
the quantum state space itself — a Hilbert space of infinite dimension,
containing every possible future geometry of spacetime.

The mind matrix at this scale is the **S-matrix**: the complete
table of amplitudes for every possible scattering outcome. Every
column represents a possible initial state; every row a possible
final state; every entry the probability amplitude for that
transition.

$N_0 = \dim(\mathcal{H}_\text{Planck}) = \infty$

The "information processing" at this scale is the collapse of this
infinite superposition into definite geometry as scales increase
and coarse-graining takes effect.
