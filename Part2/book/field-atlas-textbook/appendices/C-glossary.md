# Glossary {#app:glossary}

::: {.key-terms}
action potential
: a self-regenerating voltage pulse of about $100\,\mathrm{mV}$ that travels along a nerve fibre (@ch:cells)

albedo
: the fraction of incoming sunlight a planet reflects (@ch:earth)

aliasing
: a frequency above the Nyquist limit appearing as a lower one in sampled data (@ch:zooming)

attractor
: a state or set of states towards which nearby states evolve; a valley of a landscape (Chapters [-@ch:cells], [-@ch:human])

baryon acoustic oscillation
: the frozen imprint of sound waves in the early universe, a standard ruler of about $150\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ (@ch:cosmology)

Boltzmann factor
: $e^{-E/kT}$, the relative probability of a state of energy $E$ at temperature $T$ (Chapters [-@ch:m-chance], [-@ch:human])

boundary condition
: a rule fixing what a field does at an edge; it selects the allowed modes (Chapters [-@ch:fields], [-@ch:atoms])

causal (retarded) response
: a response that is zero before its cause (@ch:response)

closure problem
: averaged nonlinear laws need extra information about the discarded fine scale (@ch:zooming)

complex number
: a number $a + ib$ with $i^2 = -1$, drawn as a point on a plane; multiplying by $i$ is a quarter-turn (@ch:m-oscillation)

convolution
: the sum of delayed impulse responses that builds the response to any input (Chapters [-@ch:response], [-@ch:human])

critical slowing down
: the lengthening of recovery times as a system approaches a tipping point (Chapters [-@ch:cells], [-@ch:human], [-@ch:earth])

dark matter, dark energy
: mass detected only by its gravity; an energy of empty space that accelerates expansion (Chapters [-@ch:stars], [-@ch:cosmology])

density parameter $\Omega$
: a component's density as a fraction of the critical density of a flat universe (@ch:cosmology)

derivative
: the instantaneous rate of change; the slope of the tangent to a graph (@ch:m-change)

dimensionless number
: a ratio of competing effects in which all units cancel, such as the Reynolds number (@ch:zooming)

duality
: an exact dictionary mapping every observable of one theory onto another (@ch:eleven-dimensions)

eigenvalue, eigenvector
: a vector that a matrix only stretches, and the stretch factor; normal modes are eigenvectors (@ch:m-vectors)

Euler's formula
: $e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta$; an oscillation is the real part of a rotating phasor (@ch:m-oscillation)

evidence label
: the book's statement of a claim's status: `kernel-verified`, `derived-under-assumptions`, `simulated`, `empirical-result`, `interpretive` or `open-hypothesis` (@ch:fields)

falsifier
: an observation that would count against a claim (Chapters [-@ch:atoms], [-@ch:cosmology])

field
: a quantity with a value at every point of a region (@ch:fields)

flux
: the amount of a field passing through a surface (@ch:m-accumulation)

Fourier transform
: the recipe of frequencies of a signal; its modulus is the spectrum (@ch:frequencies)

gradient
: the vector of slopes of a scalar field, pointing uphill (Chapters [-@ch:m-vectors], [-@ch:fields])

Green's function (impulse response)
: a system's response to a unit kick, from which every linear response follows (@ch:response)

holonomy
: the rotation an arrow acquires when carried round a closed loop on a curved space (@ch:eleven-dimensions)

Hopfield network
: a network of threshold units whose dynamics lowers an energy, storing patterns as minima (@ch:cells)

integral
: the limit of a sum of thin strips; a signed area (@ch:m-accumulation)

Kaluza–Klein tower
: the ladder of masses $|n|\hbar c/R$ produced by a hidden circle of radius $R$ (@ch:eleven-dimensions)

Kramers escape time
: the mean time for noise to carry a state over a barrier, proportional to $e^{\Delta U/D}$ (@ch:human)

landscape
: a function whose downhill slope is the force on a state (@ch:human)

Langevin equation
: an equation of motion with a random force added (@ch:human)

length constant
: the distance over which a steady voltage falls by a factor $e$ in a passive cable (@ch:cells)

logarithm
: the power to which a base must be raised to give a number; it turns products into sums (@ch:m-scale)

look-elsewhere effect
: the more ways a match could have occurred, the less a match means (@ch:dark-sectors)

memory kernel
: the weighting with which past inputs contribute to a present state (@ch:human)

Nyquist frequency
: half the sampling rate; the highest frequency a sampled record can show (@ch:zooming)

order parameter
: a single number measuring collective order: $r$ for phases, polarisation for headings (Chapters [-@ch:groups], [-@ch:flocks])

phase-locking
: a state in which coupled oscillators run at a common frequency with a fixed lag (@ch:groups)

photon
: an indivisible packet of light with energy $hf$ (@ch:atoms)

pole
: a complex frequency at which a response becomes infinite; its real part is a ringing frequency, its imaginary part a damping rate (@ch:atoms)

power law
: a relation $y = Ax^k$; a straight line of slope $k$ on log–log axes (@ch:m-scale)

proof assistant
: a program, such as Lean, that accepts a theorem only when every step follows from definitions and axioms (@ch:evidence-proof)

propagator
: the response function of a field; its poles correspond to particles (@ch:atoms)

quality factor $Q$
: how many cycles a resonator rings, and how sharp its resonance is (@ch:response)

redshift
: $z = \Delta\lambda/\lambda$, the stretching of light by motion or by the expansion of space (Chapters [-@ch:atoms], [-@ch:cosmology])

resonance
: the large response of an oscillator driven near its natural frequency (@ch:response)

Reynolds number
: $Re = vL/\nu$, inertia compared with viscosity (@ch:zooming)

rotation curve
: orbital speed against distance from a galaxy's centre; flat curves imply dark matter (@ch:dark-sectors)

rule of three
: zero events in $n$ trials bound the rate below about $3/n$ at 95 per cent confidence (@ch:evidence-proof)

standard deviation
: the typical departure of a random quantity from its mean (Chapters [-@ch:m-chance], [-@ch:dark-sectors])

threshold
: the input above which a system's own feedback, not the input, sets the response (@ch:cells)

tipping element
: a part of the climate system that could switch to a different, hard-to-reverse state (@ch:earth)

topological interaction
: interaction with a fixed number of nearest neighbours, whatever their distance (@ch:flocks)

transfer function
: the Fourier transform of the impulse response: what a linear system does to each frequency (@ch:frequencies)

uncertainty principle
: $\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \hbar/2$; confining a wave costs momentum and energy (@ch:atoms)

zooming out (coarse-graining)
: moving up a level by averaging away detail while keeping the quantities that survive (@ch:fields)

zoom operator
: the programme's map of domain, values, operator, boundary, source and observable from one level to another (@ch:zooming)
:::
