# Appendix C: Glossary {-}

::: {.key-terms}
action potential
: a self-regenerating voltage pulse of about $100\,\mathrm{mV}$ that travels along a nerve fibre (Chapter 4)

albedo
: the fraction of incoming sunlight a planet reflects (Chapter 8)

attractor
: a state or set of states towards which nearby states evolve; a valley of a landscape (Chapters 4, 5)

baryon acoustic oscillation
: the frozen imprint of sound waves in the early universe, a standard ruler of about $150\,\mathrm{Mpc}$ (Chapter 10)

boundary condition
: a rule fixing what a field does at an edge; it selects the allowed modes (Chapters 1, 3)

causal (retarded) response
: a response that is zero before its cause (Chapter 2)

convolution
: the sum of delayed impulse responses that builds the response to any input (Chapters 2, 5)

critical slowing down
: the lengthening of recovery times as a system approaches a tipping point (Chapters 4, 5, 8)

dark matter, dark energy
: mass detected only by its gravity; an energy of empty space that accelerates expansion (Chapters 9, 10)

density parameter $\Omega$
: a component's density as a fraction of the critical density of a flat universe (Chapter 10)

evidence label
: the book's statement of a claim's status: `kernel-verified`, `derived-under-assumptions`, `simulated`, `empirical-result`, `interpretive` or `open-hypothesis` (Chapter 1)

falsifier
: an observation that would count against a claim (Chapters 3, 10)

field
: a quantity with a value at every point of a region (Chapter 1)

Green's function (impulse response)
: a system's response to a unit kick, from which every linear response follows (Chapter 2)

Hopfield network
: a network of threshold units whose dynamics lowers an energy, storing patterns as minima (Chapter 4)

Kramers escape time
: the mean time for noise to carry a state over a barrier, proportional to $e^{\Delta U/D}$ (Chapter 5)

landscape
: a function whose downhill slope is the force on a state (Chapter 5)

Langevin equation
: an equation of motion with a random force added (Chapter 5)

length constant
: the distance over which a steady voltage falls by a factor $e$ in a passive cable (Chapter 4)

memory kernel
: the weighting with which past inputs contribute to a present state (Chapter 5)

order parameter
: a single number measuring collective order: $r$ for phases, polarisation for headings (Chapters 6, 7)

phase-locking
: a state in which coupled oscillators run at a common frequency with a fixed lag (Chapter 6)

photon
: an indivisible packet of light with energy $hf$ (Chapter 3)

pole
: a complex frequency at which a response becomes infinite; its real part is a ringing frequency, its imaginary part a damping rate (Chapter 3)

propagator
: the response function of a field; its poles correspond to particles (Chapter 3)

quality factor $Q$
: how many cycles a resonator rings, and how sharp its resonance is (Chapter 2)

redshift
: $z = \Delta\lambda/\lambda$, the stretching of light by motion or by the expansion of space (Chapters 3, 10)

resonance
: the large response of an oscillator driven near its natural frequency (Chapter 2)

threshold
: the input above which a system's own feedback, not the input, sets the response (Chapter 4)

tipping element
: a part of the climate system that could switch to a different, hard-to-reverse state (Chapter 8)

topological interaction
: interaction with a fixed number of nearest neighbours, whatever their distance (Chapter 7)

uncertainty principle
: $\Delta x\,\Delta p \ge \hbar/2$; confining a wave costs momentum and energy (Chapter 3)

zooming out (coarse-graining)
: moving up a level by averaging away detail while keeping the quantities that survive (Chapter 1)
:::
