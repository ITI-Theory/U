# Appendix C: Glossary {-}

**Active matter:** moving many-body system whose parts consume energy. **Attractor:** state or region toward which nearby trajectories evolve. **Boundary condition:** rule applied at an edge or interface. **Convolution:** integral that combines a source with a Green function. **Density parameter:** cosmological contribution relative to critical density.

**Damping:** loss of organized motion from a mode. **Evidence label:** repository term that states whether a claim is kernel-verified, derived under assumptions, simulated, empirical, interpretive, or open. **Field:** value assigned over a domain. **Falsifier:** test or observation that would count against a claim. **Green function:** response to an impulse source.

**Langevin equation:** stochastic equation with deterministic drift and noise. **Memory kernel:** weighted influence of past states. **Order parameter:** summary variable for collective alignment. **Phase locking:** stable timing relation between oscillators. **Resonance:** enhanced response near a natural mode. **Zoom operator:** scale-changing registry operation that preserves specified modelling questions.
