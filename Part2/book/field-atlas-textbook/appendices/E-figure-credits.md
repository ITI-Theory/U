# Figure Credits {#app:credits}

::: {.figure-credits}
Every figure in this edition is generated from code in this repository; none is taken from a third party. Most figures in the course edition are declared in the text with the `{{Visualize}}` macro (docs/VISUALIZE.md): each names the equation it illustrates, is drawn from that equation by `lib/visualize/render.py` when the book is built, and where the text quotes a number the figure shows (an area, a slope, a flux, an eigenvalue, a probability) the renderer recomputes it and stops the build if they disagree. No figure in this book is hand-drawn or produced by an image generator. Chapter openers and chapter figures are drawn by `figures/build_figures.py`. Those that show simulations (the Hodgkin–Huxley membrane, the Langevin double well, the Kuramoto crowd, the Vicsek flock and the line of turning birds) run the stated equations at build time with fixed random seeds, so every reader's copy is identical. The figures reused from the Field Atlas (string modes, damped poles, Yukawa and Coulomb responses, and the scale and response-time plots) are generated by that book's own scripts. Galaxy points in the Hubble diagram of @ch:cosmology are simulated around the measured slope and are labelled as such. All figures are released under the repository's licence.
:::
