# Appendix E: Figure Credits {-}

::: {.figure-credits}
Every figure in this edition is generated from code in this repository; none is taken from a third party. Chapter openers and chapter figures are drawn by `figures/build_figures.py`. Those that show simulations (the Hodgkin–Huxley membrane, the Langevin double well, the Kuramoto crowd, the Vicsek flock and the line of turning birds) run the stated equations at build time with fixed random seeds, so every reader's copy is identical. The figures reused from the Field Atlas (string modes, damped poles, Yukawa and Coulomb responses, and the scale and response-time plots) are generated by that book's own scripts. Galaxy points in the Hubble diagram of Chapter 10 are simulated around the measured slope and are labelled as such. All figures are released under the repository's licence.
:::
