# Reading with the Field Atlas {#app:atlas}

The Field Atlas and this course are companion books. The Atlas is the reference and the gallery: one spread per level, with its plates, equation, scale and evidence badges, read in order from the smallest level to the largest. The course is the teaching spine: the mathematics and physics needed to read each spread, worked through with examples and homework. The table below is the bridge between them. Reading the Atlas from level 1 to level 31, keep the course open at the chapters listed for each level; reading a course chapter, the same table says which Atlas spreads show its subject. @ch:fields and the toolkit of Part 0 underlie every row.

The mapping is kept as data (`atlas-map.yaml`) and the level names and sizes are read from the same registry that builds the Atlas and the Soma Machine, so the three cannot drift apart. A level missing from the map stops the build.
