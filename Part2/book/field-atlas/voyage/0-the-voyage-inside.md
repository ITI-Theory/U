title: The Voyage Inside
---

Sector III ends where the atlas leaves the single body for the pair, the group and the city. This part turns the other way: into one body, and into the brain inside it. Its plates are the stops of the Soma Machine's mind-body explorer, captured from the app with its controls removed, and each comes with the text that says what it shows and what it does not.

The rule is the atlas's own. The anatomy is drawn schematically and labelled where ordinary science supplies it [@openstax2022anatomy; @nummenmaa2014bodily; @ledoux1996emotional] `sourced`. The moving parts are small simulations with stated rules: a branching process, a Hopfield network, coupled oscillators, a gradient flow on an energy landscape `simulated`. The field that runs through all of them, the "second nervous system" of the model, is the programme's hypothesis, not a finding `open-hypothesis`. Nothing here is a medical display or a model of any patient.

The voyage has an order. It starts with the whole person, goes into the body and up the arteries into the brain, through the brain's own cave to the place where a feeling becomes a thought, down to one neuron and one memory, out to the landscape of states and the pair, along the path of a thought, and ends with a stone in a ureter: the same barrier the theory talks about, met in the body. Four halls are its crossroads, as a game's hub worlds are: the heart's atrium, the brain's great hall, the mind chamber and the soma of a neuron, each opening off a floor of the level ladder, with doorways to the stops around it. Their anatomy is named where it is real; their light and proportions are for the eye `interpretive`.
