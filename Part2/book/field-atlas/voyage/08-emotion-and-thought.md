title: Emotion and Thought
---

Below the line in the plate is the sub-threshold sea: field activity that is real and acts on the body but is not felt, the model's subconscious. Triggers from the body raise a swell. When a swell crosses the threshold it condenses into an emotion in the limbic band; if it keeps rising it becomes a thought in the cortex band. An emotion and a thought are the same field at different types, as in the Gestalt paper's reading of Russell's theory of types [@P17] `interpretive`.

The threshold itself is the one formal fact here: Lean checks that every amplitude is either below the model's threshold or at or above it (`consciousness_dichotomy`), an order fact about real numbers and nothing more `kernel-verified`. That crossing it is becoming conscious is the hypothesis `open-hypothesis`. A thought also sends a pulse back down to the body; with the limbic field high, the loop can spiral, which is the model's picture of rumination.

Emotion and thought as types of one field: swells of the sub-threshold sea rise through the threshold plane into emotions, and the strongest on to thoughts at the cortex level (`interpretive`; the threshold order fact is `kernel-verified`).
:::

Through the limbic gate the same picture becomes a room, the mind chamber: the gold plane is the threshold, the sea below it the field that acts without being felt, and each sphere that rises above it a feeling, then a thought. A sphere shows how it is expressed (a bright core for an emotion, fused spheres for a blend, a clamped one for one damped down, a spark for the mechanism that evoked it); with the model's C-PTSD operator some stay up instead of falling back [@P13] `interpretive`.

From the chamber a doorway opens on THE COURSE, the inside of a thought that sticks ("should I go skiing on Saturday?"). It opens into a field of small holes, each one the landscape stop's SAFE, FIGHT and FREEZE wells in miniature, swelling like a sea under a glass dome, the threshold. The considerations arrive one after another (the weather, when it gets dark, rain tomorrow), each a ripple across the course that settles the holes it passes: a hole's height is its cost on the energy landscape, its colour the feeling, warm red for fear and anger, cool blue for joy and trust. Low and blue is an obvious yes, high and red an obvious no; stress is height and colour disagreeing. A consideration that reaches the glass passes through it as a thought, a few at a time. Either the course settles, and the easiest way through is drawn across it, or it does not, and the thought sinks back below the threshold and stays. The course is a picture of the model's terms, not of a brain `interpretive`.
