---
title: "The Score: One Film, Four Containers"
---

The last stop is a film. *The Tensor* defines a film not by its pictures but by an emotional score: seven modes (safety, fear, curiosity, awe, grief, language and the pre-verbal) over story-time, with thresholds where the film holds until the viewer is ready [@P8]. The score is the invariant; what carries it is a container. The paper's own example is *Heart of Darkness* and *Apocalypse Now*: one upriver score, two rivers `interpretive`.

The explorer plays one score in four containers, its trilogy and a frame: the mind, as a Mandelbulb whose power, light and colour follow the modes; the river, a boat going upriver; the body, a submarine in the bloodstream; and the session, client and therapist. The four plates show the same story-time, just past the fear peak, in each setting: the same thresholds at the same moments, the pictures different. The somatic loop that slows the film when the viewer's heart rises, and the viewer's own version beside the film, belong to the app and are not printed here. Whether such a score moves a viewer as the model says is `open-hypothesis`.
