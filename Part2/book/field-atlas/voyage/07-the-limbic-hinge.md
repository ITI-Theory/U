title: The Limbic Hinge
---

In the Soma-Field model the limbic system is dimension 8: the hinge between the body field (dimensions 1 to 7) and the mind field (9 to 11). Signals flow from the body through the limbic band to the mind, and calming signals come back from the cortex. One number, the limbic field Φ, sets the temperature of the mind's network, T = T₀ + σΦ: hot, and its picture blurs while the calming signals thin [@P13] `open-hypothesis`.

This is where the story of the programme turns. Trauma therapies that work with the body aim here, at the alarm rather than only at the thoughts [@levine2010; @ogden2006] `sourced`; the model gives that aim a coordinate. Whether the brain has such a hinge in this form is the hypothesis the model is built to test.
