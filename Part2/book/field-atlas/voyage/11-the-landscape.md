---
title: The Landscape of States
---

The mountains of the explorer are an energy landscape: every possible state of the eight emotional modes has an energy, the valleys are attractors, and the ball is where a person's state is now [@P1; @P17]. The terrain in the plate is computed from the same energy as the checked simulation, and the ball follows its gradient flow with noise `simulated`.

The limbic field is the temperature. Raised, it melts the valleys and the ball can cross a ridge it could not cross cold; at 11D the model adds tunnelling through a barrier rather than over it [@P2] `open-hypothesis`. A valley the ball cannot leave is the model's fixed gestalt; the ridges between valleys are the boundaries the next stops are about.
