title: The Cathedral
---

The brain has a cave of its own: the ventricles, linked chambers full of cerebrospinal fluid, from the fourth ventricle by the brainstem up the narrow aqueduct to the third ventricle and out through the interventricular foramen into the two lateral ventricles [@openstax2022anatomy] `sourced`. The explorer flies them as a cathedral: stone walls, curtains for the choroid plexus that makes the fluid, light for the field `interpretive`.

The three plates show the same cave three ways. Inside, the fourth ventricle opens ahead. Outside, the chambers are one shape inside the brain. With the effects turned off, the honest drawing remains: the ventricles in side view, labelled in the order the cave is flown, at schematic positions rather than measured ones. The first two are pictures; the third is the anatomy they are pictures of.
