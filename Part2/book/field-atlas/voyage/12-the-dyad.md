---
title: The Dyad
---

Two people, two rhythms: heart and breath, each drawn as one phase. Coupled across the contact boundary, the rhythms can lock, as weakly coupled oscillators do when their natural rates are close enough [@kuramoto1984chemical; @acebron2005kuramoto] `sourced`. In the plate the limbic field widens the gap between the two rates, and a resource strengthens the coupling; in step, the shared field around both is bright `simulated`.

The clinical reading belongs to the dyad level of the atlas: attunement, rupture when the two drift apart, and repair when they lock again, the co-regulation that therapy relies on [@P3] `interpretive`. The plate shows the mechanism, not a measurement of any pair.
