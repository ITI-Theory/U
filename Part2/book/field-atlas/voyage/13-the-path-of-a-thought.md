---
title: The Path of a Thought
---

Boundaries are paths. The plate draws a thought as a ball racing along a tunnel through the brain, with each boundary a gate on its path: a gate opens when the limbic field is above its height, and a closed gate stops the thought `simulated`. Where a gate is closed the thought can still try to tunnel through, with a chance that falls exponentially with the height of the gap but never reaches zero; Lean checks that the WKB amplitude behind that chance is positive (`wkbAmplitude_pos`) `kernel-verified`.

Which path a thought takes is set by which gates are open, which is the model's reading of how a state finds, or fails to find, a way out [@P25] `open-hypothesis`. The walls are the fractal of the level and the Mandelbulb at the centre is the mind: pictures around a simulated rule `interpretive`.
