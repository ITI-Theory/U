The voyage begins with the humanoid of the level views, the human as the Soma Machine draws it at 11D: a translucent body with its fields around it and the brain on top. The limbic field, Φ, is the one control that matters here; as it rises the figure glows. The figure is a picture of the model, not of anatomy `interpretive`.

The explorer is a view inside the same machine as the level ladder: the human level opens the voyage, and each later stop names the level it belongs to (the human, the whole brain, the cellular level, the dyad). The layers are the atlas's own: 4D is the body as physics knows it, 8D its regulation and signalling, 11D the field the model adds [@P11; @P20].

Before the human there is the map. THE WORLDS draws the zoom as glass domes on a dark plain, one dome per stretch of the ladder, from the quantum foam to the cosmic web; the voyage goes through the glass of the human dome. The domes are a map of the levels, not places in the universe: worlds nest inside worlds in the picture only, and the territory is the ladder of length scales the atlas's sectors set out `interpretive`.
