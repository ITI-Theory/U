title: The Way In
---

The way into the brain is by blood. The plate is a schematic arterial voyage from the heart: up the aorta, into the common and then the internal carotid artery, round the circle of Willis at the base of the brain, and out along the middle cerebral artery. The labels give approximate calibres, a few centimetres at the aorta down to a few millimetres, so the tube narrows as the voyage climbs [@openstax2022anatomy] `sourced`.

It is anatomy for orientation, drawn rather than imaged, and nothing in it is a clinical display. The pulse seen on the way is the same heartbeat as at the start, now as flow through smaller vessels. The arrival is the brain.
