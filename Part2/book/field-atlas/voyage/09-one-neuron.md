title: One Neuron
---

The voyage zooms to one cell. The dendrites on the left gather input, the cell body decides, the axon on the right sends; myelin wraps the axon, and the spikes jump from gap to gap along it [@openstax2022anatomy] `sourced`. The trace in the plate is the membrane voltage of a simple model neuron whose firing rate rises with the limbic field `simulated`.

Each spike adds a little to the electric field around the axon, and that field can nudge a neighbouring cell: ephaptic coupling, small but measured in brain tissue [@anastassiou2011ephaptic] `sourced`. Whether such fields matter for experience is the open question the 11D layer carries `open-hypothesis`.

:::

Inside the cell body is the neuron's hall, the soma. The nucleus sits in the middle with its nucleolus; the walls are stacked rough endoplasmic reticulum, the Nissl substance, where the cell makes its proteins; mitochondria supply its energy, and microtubules run towards the axon hillock, where a spike begins [@openstax2022anatomy] `sourced`. Proportions and light are for the eye `interpretive`; the doorways go to the hillock, out along the dendrites to memory, into the sea of many cells, and up to the great hall.

:::
