# Open questions

This atlas is a map of a research programme, not a record of settled doctrine. The open questions below are kept because they can change the map.

**`simulated` Independent replication of QUANT-EXP-1.** The present result is an exact 8-qubit statevector simulation of an annealing-style reachability problem. It is not a device result and not evidence for a clinical mechanism. The direct test is simple: an independent group should reimplement the Hamiltonian, schedules, seeds, and barrier cases, then compare the reported reachability rates with confidence intervals. A negative replication would be as informative as a positive one.

**`open-hypothesis` Device execution.** If the simulation reproduces, the next question is whether a comparable instance can run on real quantum devices. Annealing-style and gate-model implementations test different engineering routes. Success would show that the computational mechanism is physically realisable in a device; it would still not show that living tissue uses it.

**`open-hypothesis` Microtubule coherence in living tissue.** The Penrose-Hameroff substrate remains controversial because warm, wet biological coherence is difficult to measure. The discriminating experiment is an in vivo or near-physiological coherence-time measurement with clear controls, temperature reporting, and independent replication. Without that, microtubules remain a candidate substrate rather than a demonstrated one.

**`open-hypothesis` Catastrophic-fold clinical transitions.** The programme proposes that some abrupt state changes can be modelled with catastrophe geometry. This is not clinical advice and no funded clinical study is running. A pilot could test the geometry by following consenting participants over time with preregistered physiological and report-based measures, then asking whether transitions fit fold or cusp models better than smooth drift.

**`open-hypothesis` Group coupling in music and collective affect.** The music paper predicts that non-uniform groups may synchronise most strongly when music supplies an integrative perturbation. This can be tested with HRV, movement, and timing data across rehearsals or concerts, comparing synchrony before, during, and after shared stimuli against matched control events.

**`open-hypothesis` The eightfold mode structure.** The eight named modes are a useful model grammar, not a proven taxonomy of affective state. A consensus-elicitation study with trained clinicians, followed by blinded coding of state descriptions, could test whether eight clusters outperform six-, seven-, or continuous-dimensional alternatives.

**`kernel-verified`/`derived-under-assumptions` Proof-surface closure.** Lean 4 checks formal statements under definitions and axioms. The current surface uses Lean and Mathlib v4.33.0 and includes 20 axioms and five real `sorry`s. The test is technical: close the remaining gaps, minimise axiom dependence, and state theorem provenance precisely. Even a fully closed proof surface would verify mathematics, not automatically verify empirical interpretation.

**`open-hypothesis` Compact $G_2$ geometry and the origin of eight.** The proposed relation between M-theory compactification, $G_2$ geometry, and soma-field modes depends on mathematical and interpretive assumptions. A decisive advance would be a constructive compact $G_2$ example with the required singularity and moduli structure, or a proof that the required structure cannot exist.

**`open-hypothesis` Scale invariance across the whole ladder.** The recurring Green's-function grammar is a disciplined comparison, not proof that every level is the same system. The test is predictive: each level should specify observables, parameter ranges, and failure conditions. Levels that cannot generate discriminating predictions should remain pedagogical, not evidential.
