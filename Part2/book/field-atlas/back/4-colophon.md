# Colophon

*[T]-Theory: Field Atlas — The Wave That Is Always There* is an independently published Zurich 2026 edition by Alistair Johnson, ORCID 0009-0007-2194-0850. It is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): readers may copy, share, adapt, and build on the work, including commercially, provided attribution is given and changes are indicated.

This edition is built from the same registry as the Soma Machine app. The level names, characteristic scales, sectors, lenses, evidence badges, and picture instructions are therefore shared between the printed atlas and the interactive view at t-theory.org. The atlas fixes selected spreads for slow reading; the app exposes the same material as a navigable console.

The text distinguishes formal statements, sourced ordinary science, model-derived claims, and interpretive hypotheses. It is a research atlas, not clinical guidance, and not a claim that all levels of nature are literally one substance. Its purpose is to make the programme inspectable scale by scale.
