Sector III covers the move from a whole brain to a whole organism, then from one organism to two, and finally to many. Its ordinary science is heterogeneous. Brain-scale CEMI discussion begins with neural currents, synchrony, and measured electromagnetic fields, while remaining cautious about causal consciousness claims [@mcfadden2002a; @mcfadden2002b]. The human/vertebrate level adds interoception, fascia, posture, heart and respiratory timing, endocrine and autonomic regulation, and environmental forcing [@craig2003; @ingber1997; @grossman2007toward]. Dyads introduce synchronisation and interaction timing, for which coupled-oscillator and phase-locking models are useful but limited [@acebron2005kuramoto; @tschacher2014nonverbal]. Animal swarms then replace pair labels with density, alignment, velocity fields, and noise [@vicsek1995novel; @toner1995long; @bialek2012statistical].

The sector's central plate is the human level. In the programme it is where affect is modelled as a field, where a Hopfield-style attractor landscape becomes the grammar for eight labelled organism states, and where music, rhythm, posture, memory, and touch can enter as forcing terms `interpretive`. *The Missing Limbic Layer: A Somatic Field Extension of Hopfield Networks via the Correspondence Principle* makes this a modelling centre, not a clinical fact or proof of consciousness `derived-under-assumptions`. *The Soma-Field: A Wave-Based Model of Emotional Dynamics* and *The Physical Substrate of the Soma-Field* motivate the organismal substrate while keeping substrate identity open `open-hypothesis`.

The main physics that carries through the sector is response. A source perturbs a bounded system; the system answers through a kernel, damping term, attractor, phase-locking condition, or velocity field `interpretive`. What changes at each zoom is the variable that makes the response visible. CEMI needs fields over neural tissue; the human plate needs an organism state vector; the dyad needs off-diagonal delay; the swarm needs coarse-grained alignment and density `derived-under-assumptions`.

The open questions are correspondingly strict. Which electromagnetic or interoceptive variables matter causally? Which eight-mode coordinates are measurable? When does dyadic synchrony help, harm, or simply describe timing? How far can active-matter equations travel before species biology breaks the analogy? The bird side-path--bird, flock, colony-roost--is included as a concrete named solution: one avian body becomes an active flock, then a site-level continuity pattern. It illustrates the same zoom grammar without importing human affect into birds or colonies `open-hypothesis`.
