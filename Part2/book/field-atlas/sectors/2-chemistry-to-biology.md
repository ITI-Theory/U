Sector II follows the scale band where chemical structure becomes living signalling. At the molecular level, the relevant physics is quantum and electromagnetic: nuclei and electron clouds form bonds, proteins fold, ligands bind, and conformational changes alter function. At the cellular-synaptic level, those molecular events are enclosed by membranes and converted into bioelectric variables: voltage, ion gradients, calcium, receptor state, vesicle release, and gene-regulatory feedback. At the local-circuit level, many excitable cells become a mesoscopic network with recurrence, inhibition, delay, and phase. At the whole-brain-CEMI level, distributed source currents form measurable electric and magnetic fields, while the stronger claim that such fields integrate conscious content remains a hypothesis [@mcfadden2002a; @mcfadden2002b].

The ordinary science is therefore a sequence of controlled losses. Orbitals and bond vibrations matter for molecules, but a circuit cannot track every orbital. Ion-channel kinetics matter for cells, but a whole-brain field cannot track every channel. Each zoom preserves what still changes the next scale: thresholds, coupling strengths, timing, source currents, boundary conditions, and response kernels. The governing equations in the registry are deliberately compact displays: Schrödinger form for molecular state, cable equation for membrane voltage, damped wave equation for circuit propagation, and Helmholtz-style Green's-function response for brain-scale fields. They are not interchangeable proofs. They are scale-specific ways of asking how a source produces a response.

The main open questions sit exactly at the boundaries. Developmental reaction--diffusion patterns show that biology can make form from local rules [@turing1952morphogenesis], and electrophysiology shows how membranes can compute with ions [@hodgkin1952quantitative; @rall1962theory]. But it remains unsettled how much endogenous electromagnetic coupling contributes to cognition, how far CEMI theories can be tested against competing global-workspace or integrated-information accounts, and whether microtubules have any cognitive quantum role beyond ordinary cell biology. Hameroff--Penrose Orch OR is therefore kept only as an `open-hypothesis`, with decoherence objections explicitly visible [@tegmark2000importance; @hameroff2014consciousness].

The carrying idea for the sector is response grammar. A perturbation enters a substrate; the substrate filters, delays, amplifies, damps, or thresholds it; the next scale sees the surviving response, not the full microscopic history. In [T]-Theory this cross-scale grammar is `interpretive`, with some formal statements `derived-under-assumptions` inside *The Universal Somatic Field: Green's Functions as Scale-Invariant Oscillators across Twenty Scale Levels*. No level in this sector is claimed to prove consciousness, therapy, or feeling in isolated matter.
