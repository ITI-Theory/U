# The mind palace

The oldest memory technique is a building. Ancient orators placed each thing to be remembered in a room of an imagined palace and walked through it to recall them in order: the method of loci [@yates1966art] `sourced`. The Soma Machine builds such a palace from the atlas itself. Its floors are the zoom dial's twenty-one levels, the universal-21 model of the registry, from the quantum foam at the bottom to the cosmic web at the top. Each floor has one door, into its level, and a reader can leave a thought on a floor and come back to find it.

The same tower is the elevator of the level view, the lift whose car rides the app's zoom: floor by floor the length scale changes by powers of ten while the response grammar stays on top, unchanged [@P11]. Below are two of its halls as the explorer draws them; overleaf is the whole tower, drawn from the registry, every floor with its scale and its sector. The palace is a building for the mind, not a claim about the cosmos `interpretive`. It is printed here, before the levels, because it is the shape of the whole atlas in one picture.
