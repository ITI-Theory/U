The ladder is not a claim that every level is governed by one literal substance. It is a navigation device. At each step, the reader should ask which variables remain visible, which details are integrated out, and which new collective quantities become necessary. The sector names are editorial conveniences, not hard walls: molecular systems lead into living networks, organismal regulation feeds collective coordination, and planetary dynamics belong both to geology and to orbital physics. The bird--flock--roost side-path demonstrates this overlap explicitly. It follows one organism through a named solution of coordination before returning to the broader social and systemic ladder. The atlas therefore reads vertically, as a zoom sequence, and laterally, as a set of neighbouring models that share response grammar without becoming identical.
