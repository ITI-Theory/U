The ladder is not a claim that every level is governed by one literal substance. It is a navigation device. At each step, the reader should ask which variables remain visible, which details are integrated out, and which new quantities become necessary. The sector names are editorial conveniences, not hard walls: molecular systems lead into living networks, organismal regulation feeds dyads and groups, group coordination neighbours animal active matter, and planetary dynamics belong both to geology and to orbital physics.

Two numbering models may appear in associated materials. The book uses five canonical sectors, sometimes indexed 0--5 for interface convenience. Some programme papers also use a universal 0--20 ladder convention. Those numbers are secondary. The registry names in this table are authoritative for the atlas and the Soma Machine app. The bird--flock--roost side-path demonstrates overlap explicitly, and the path chapter explains how dyadic care, performance, assembly, engineered agents, geology, and cosmic routes reuse the same level names without making neighbouring systems identical.
