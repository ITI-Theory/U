## Sourcing rules

1. **Default to public domain.** NASA, ESA, USGS, NOAA, NIH, Wikimedia
   Commons CC0, swisstopo Open Government Data. These never need clearance.
2. **CC BY / CC BY-SA** is fine; we credit per CREDITS.md and respect
   share-alike.
3. **Fair use** of small textbook/journal figures only where (a) they are
   indispensable historical objects (Hopfield 1982 figure, etc.) and (b) we
   reduce, recaption, and credit clearly. Limit to ≤ 5 such figures total.
4. **Permission requests** for Levin biotensegrity, Guimberteau fascia, Popp
   biophoton: send via email, log response in CREDITS.md. If declined,
   substitute with our own redraw.
5. **Drone shots (Glarus)** — Alistair's originals. Reserve highest-resolution
   copies in `figures/source/` (gitignored), commit only the print-resolution
   exports.

## Conventions

* All BUILD figures live as `<id>.py` + generated `<id>.png` (300 dpi) +
  `<id>.pdf` (vector where possible).
* Captions live in the chapter markdown next to the figure; this file is the
  inventory and licence ledger only.
* On every figure update, re-run `make figures-credits` to regenerate
  `back-matter-credits.md` from this table.
