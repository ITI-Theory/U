In 1966 and 1967 Andy Warhol's *Exploding Plastic Inevitable* put the Velvet Underground on a stage and threw film and coloured light onto them as they played: the band became the screen. The band of those years is remembered in black and white, in Warhol's sixteen-millimetre screen tests and the photographs of the Factory.

The programme's first notes asked for the same thing: a sheet to project onto, and music to drive the picture. So this room leaves the programme's clock for that one. The pictures are the Soma Machine's, filmed as 1966 filmed: hard contrast, grain, a vignette, a scratch.
