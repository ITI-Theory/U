1 October 2026: the philosophy book begins. It follows Bertrand Russell's *History of Western Philosophy* (1945), whose order is time and whose questions do not age.

From 1910 to 1913 Russell and Whitehead published *Principia Mathematica*: mathematics built up from logic, on a ladder of types, so that no collection can contain itself. In Munich, in December 1911, Kandinsky and Marc opened the first Blaue Reiter exhibition, and Kandinsky's *On the Spiritual in Art* came out: colour as an inner sound.

So the clock goes back to 1911. The palace, a thought and a score are painted in flat colour and black line: Kandinsky's palette, never his pictures. The forms are the programme's own.
