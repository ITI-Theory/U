Leibniz hoped that disagreements might one day be settled the way sums are: *calculemus*, let us sit down and calculate. In 1714, two years before his death, he wrote the *Monadology*: a world of simple substances, each a living mirror of the whole universe from its own point of view.

Learning Lean is a small, real piece of that hope: a statement type-checks or it does not. So the clock goes back to 1714, and the city of code is engraved, as a plate in a book of his time.
