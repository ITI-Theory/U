The first dated notes, in April and May 2026, are not about physics. They are about a room: black gauze a projector can paint on and still be seen through, a four-metre sheet of white spandex, a small DLP projector, an Ableton Push. A space for light and sound, with a person inside it.

In the same weeks: how a hacker's tools are made (by hand, kept simple), and how to write a knowledge base of common sense in Lean.

The pictures in this room are first drawings, pencil on paper. The pink and the blue the programme will later wear are already in the strokes, faintly, fitting in but not quite.
