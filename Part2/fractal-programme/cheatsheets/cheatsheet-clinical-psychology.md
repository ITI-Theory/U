# I · The Trauma Well

The Hopfield energy landscape:
$$H(\Psi) = -\tfrac{1}{2}\Psi^T W_8\,\Psi$$

A trauma memory = a stored pattern $\Psi^*$ with deep well $H(\Psi^*)$.
Trauma activation = the field rolling into the well from a trigger.

**Escape probability** (Kramers formula analog):
$$P_\mathrm{escape} \propto e^{-\Delta H / T_\mathrm{field}}$$

Therapy increases $T_\mathrm{field}$ (arousal window) or reshapes $\Delta H$
(changing the well geometry via new associative memory).
