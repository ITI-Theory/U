# I · The Rapport Propagator

Rapport between agents $A$ and $B$ is the two-point function:
$$\langle\Psi_A(t)\,\Psi_B(t')\rangle = G_\mathrm{rapport}(t-t')$$

The correlation decays as $e^{-|t-t'|/\tau_\mathrm{social}}$ where
$\tau_\mathrm{social}$ is the social memory timescale.

Strong rapport = low $k_9$ (long correlation length in social space).
Weak rapport = high $k_9$ (rapid decorrelation).
Collective intelligence = correlated field achieving $k_9 \to 0$.

# II · Structural Contagion

Emotional contagion in cities (dialect spread, fashion, opinion):
$$\frac{\partial\Psi}{\partial t} = D\nabla^2\Psi - k^2\Psi + J_\mathrm{media}(x,t)$$

This is the \textbf{diffusion-reaction equation} for social fields.
The wavenumber $k_9$ sets the social correlation length $\ell = 1/k_9$.
At scale 9, $\ell$ ranges from a city block (local dialect) to a continent
(global cultural attractor).
