# I · The Mandate Consensus Propagator

The collective decision field in a democratic system:
$$\Psi_\mathrm{pol}(\mathbf{x},t) = \sum_i w_i\,\Psi_i(t)\,\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}_i)$$

The \textbf{mandate} at time $t$ = the dominant eigenmode of
the collective field $\Psi_\mathrm{pol}$.
Consensus = when one eigenmode captures $>50\%$ of field energy.

The propagator of democratic consensus:
$$G_\mathrm{mandate}(\tau) = e^{-\tau/\tau_\mathrm{mandate}}\cos(\omega_\mathrm{election}\,\tau)$$

$\tau_\mathrm{mandate}$ = political mandate lifetime (days to years);
$\omega_\mathrm{election}$ = electoral cycle frequency.

# II · Policy as Field Perturbation

A policy intervention $P$ perturbs the collective field:
$$\delta\Psi_\mathrm{pol}(t) = G_\mathrm{mandate} \ast J_P(t)$$

Short-term policy impact = the field response over $\tau \ll \tau_\mathrm{mandate}$.
Long-term institutional change = basin reshaping (structural reform).

The political philosopher's problem: design $J_P(t)$ to move the
collective field toward a desired attractor, with minimal coercion.

# III · The Three Disciplines as One Field

\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{Politics}: how to reshape the basin? (dynamics of $\nabla H$)
\item \textbf{Economics}: what is the cost of each trajectory? (action $S$)
