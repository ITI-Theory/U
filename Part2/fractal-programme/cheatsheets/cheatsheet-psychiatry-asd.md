# I · The Clinical Operator Propagator

The resolvent of the Hopfield Hamiltonian in the clinical parameter regime:
$$G_\mathrm{clinical}(\lambda) = (H - \lambda I)^{-1}$$

Poles of $G_\mathrm{clinical}$ at eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of $W_8$ = normal modes
of the emotional processing system. Clinical presentation = which pole
dominates the dynamics.

DSM-5 categories $\approx$ attractor basin topology classifications:
disordered configurations of the same 8-dimensional field.

# II · Neurodevelopmental Phenotypes

The Pre-Verbal Manifold (P18): neurodevelopmental phenotypes
arise from altered $W_8$ structure set during early field formation:

\begin{itemize}
\item ASD: reduced Contagion (CO) weight → social field coupling deficit
\item ADHD: high temporal $k$ → rapid basin switching, poor well retention
\item Twice-exceptional: multiple deep wells with high $T_\mathrm{field}$ → creative instability
\end{itemize}

These are \textbf{field geometries}, not deficits. Different landscapes
support different cognitive styles.

# III · The Limbic Axis in Psychiatry

The CO (Contagion) mechanism maps to the Limbic Axis $D_8 = S^1/\mathbb{Z}_2$
in the USF geometry. The two boundary branes correspond to:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Sympathetic activation}: field temperature rises, escape probability increases
\item \textbf{Parasympathetic regulation}: field cools, basin stabilises
\end{itemize}
The therapeutic window = the field operating above the sympathetic floor
and below the parasympathetic ceiling.

# IV · Falsifiable Predictions
