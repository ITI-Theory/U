# Credits: sound and pictures from outside the programme

Everything the Soma Machine plays or shows that the programme did not make is listed here,
with its licence, before it is used (ISS-073). Today the music is generated from the score
(`explorer-music.js`) and the MIDI goes to the author's own instruments: nothing here yet.

## Rules for a fixed piece of music

1. The composition in the public domain (the composer died over 70 years ago, e.g. Satie,
   1925) **and** the recording itself free: CC0 or public domain (e.g. Musopen's own
   recordings marked so). A free composition in a copyrighted recording is not free.
2. The source page and its licence statement saved with the entry (date checked).
3. No AI-generated songs (licences and training data unclear; ISS-066's lesson).
4. No SpaceEngine music: its licence excludes the soundtrack (SpaceEngine PRO Licence
   Agreement v1.8, sections 3.1.1 and 4.2), and effects added in Ableton would make a
   derivative of it.

## Rules for SpaceEngine pictures

Screenshots, video, textures and models rendered with the author's SpaceEngine PRO licence
may be used, commercially too, with this credit in the work and here:

> Produced in part with SpaceEngine PRO © Cosmographic Software LLC

Check the current licence before each release.
