A WORMHOLE

SOURCED A Morris-Thorne wormhole (here the simplest, Ellis's): an exact solution of Einstein's equations, at the compact-object level. A solution of the equations; none observed; a traversable one needs exotic matter (negative energy density).

The picture is ray-traced through the metric: every pixel's light bends round the throat. Rays that pass wider than the throat return to our sky (blue); narrower ones go through and show the other side's (gold). Drag to look round.

The embedding diagram: the wormhole's space, one dimension dropped, as a surface; the dot is you. After Ellis (1973), Morris and Thorne (1988); the ray-tracing after James, von Tunzelmann, Franklin and Thorne (2015).
