# The Soma Machine: the design (15 Oct 2026)

The app, not the printed books, is the programme's show piece (author, 15 Oct). This is the
design it is built to, drawn up by Copilot from a conversation with HAL-UAT (the notebook of the
author's chats; uat/RC3/hal-final-design-2026-10-15-answers.md and -q5-answers.md), checked
against the Lean, for the author's review. Decisions are the author's; open ones are listed last.

## What it is

An instrument, not a simulator: the user is the machine, and the pilot. Two parts are physical
and educational: the elevator, a physics model of one field at 21 scales (the quantum foam to the
cosmic web), and the body and brain, biology. The third part, the mind, is the model's: what a
thought, a feeling, a pain, a flashback is, drawn only from what the model says (the visual
grammar, below), interpretive and labelled so.

## One machine: places, views, controls, settings

Today the app feels like two apps (the levels view and the mind-body explorer) and the explorer's
two rows of thirty-odd buttons mix four kinds of thing. Every element is one of four:

- **Place**: where you are. The elevator shaft (21 floors); the jellyfish human (floor 8); the
  atriums, chambers seen from inside (the brain's great hall, the heart's left atrium, the
  neuron's soma); the tubes between them (arteries, ventricles, nerves, the ureter); the Mind
  Chamber.
- **View**: how you look at the place you are in (the 3D chamber, a cutaway, the field's glow;
  4D, 8D, 11D, where 11D shows the manifold beneath the walls; stereo).
- **Control**: what you do now (POKE: a push on the field, J_user; SNAP and SNAP + SAY; DRIVE
  along a tube or a path; STEP OUT and STEP IN).
- **Setting**: how the world is tuned, set once and left (Φ, the neurotype operators, the
  viewer preset, sound, vividness): the film stock, recorded with every somagram.

## The Mind Chamber

Reached from the brain's great hall through the limbic gate: physical tubes end, the mind's
chamber begins (HAL: an interpretive unification of the author's three caves, the alpine
amphitheatre, the fractal foam and the Fantastic Voyage interior; it keeps the model's order,
body, limbic hinge, mind). The author's picture (15 Oct): dark; a foam that bubbles; now and
then one sparks into reality as a sphere and drifts away; with complex PTSD some stay, rising a
little and holding, twelve or thirty of them; and paths leaving for a plan's options.

- **The foam**: the field below the threshold, real but not felt (preconscious).
- **The threshold**: a gold plane across the chamber (T_c = √2, fixed in the model: shown, not a
  slider). A swell that crosses it becomes conscious.
- **The layer**: low in the chamber an event is a feeling (D8), a pulsing swell; lifted (D9-11)
  it condenses into a sphere: one event read at two levels.
- **The sphere shows its expression** (EmotionOntology: blend, dampen, evoke over joy, trust,
  fear, surprise, sadness, disgust, anger, anticipation): each basic emotion a glowing core in its
  colour; blend, two cores fused in phase (love = joy with trust); dampen, one radiant core with
  the other clamped in a dark lattice (contempt = disgust holding anger down); evoke, the spark
  that births the sphere, one signature per mechanism (a lightning strike for the brain stem, a
  thread snapping from a scar on the wall for episodic memory, a rolling wave for entrainment, a
  folding crystal for aesthetic judgement).
- **Five kinds of sphere** (the author's words heard, pictures seen, feelings, pain,
  flashbacks): a feeling, pure cores pulsing with the heart; words, typographic rings; a picture,
  a lens; pain, jagged and crimson, tethered to its place in the body; a flashback, trapped.
- **Thoughts that stay**: attractors whose barrier is high (C-PTSD): they rise a little and hold
  behind crystalline ridges; POKE tries to tunnel (the amplitude falls with the barrier: light
  bleeding through the ridge).
- **Temperature**: Φ heats the chamber: the foam boils faster, the shimmer rises.
- **Paths**: tubes leaving the chamber, one per option; travelled ones are grooved (the route
  matters: path-sensitive transitions).
- **Echo and feedback**: a thought reverberates off the walls (the memory kernel, C-PTSD's echo)
  and sends a pulse down to the heart (the somatic loop), which stirs more foam.
- **Going in**: a click on a sphere dives into its own fractal world (built, 14 Oct).

## The process of thought (agreed 15 Oct)

In the Mind Chamber thoughts pop and vanish, a wave, many at once. One sticks: a click (or the
voice of someone talking) puts it in focus, and the chamber blurs but carries on. It opens in place
into THE COURSE: hundreds of SAFE / FIGHT / FREEZE holes, so many they read as a surface, under a
glass dome (the threshold; the global workspace, Baars, Dehaene). Considerations ripple across it
(height the cost, colour the feeling); the strong ones pop out over the dome, at most four held at
once; SOLUTION (an insight) melts them back down. If it does not resolve, the thought sinks below
the line and stays, and the pile is the load: the chamber runs hotter (allostatic load; the
Zeigarnik effect, suggestive). Thoughts are soap bubbles, changing shape as they drift. The PATH
stop is THE WAY UP: a signal from the body to the cortex, the physical road, not a thought.

**A model gap: the inner voice.** The little voice is inner speech (Vygotsky); in the brain, the
language network (left inferior frontal and superior temporal cortex, the supplementary motor
area), speech predicted and not spoken. The author's hypothesis that it becomes conscious by
joining the brain's electromagnetic field is CEMI (McFadden), the model's whole-brain level: a
labelled open hypothesis, and classical (the brain's field is low-frequency and warm; not quantum).
Papers first (with ISS-101's frequency axis: gamma synchrony and conscious access).

## The visual grammar

Every picture of the mind expresses a model term, or says it is decorative; every term is pinned
to the Lean (registry/visual-grammar.yaml; `make -C sherlock grammar`; release check 19;
Moriarty plants a chocolate bar and a renamed Lean name, both caught). Today 9 of 12 terms are

mechanism (the spark), the route (the paths).

## Build order

1. **The spine**: one shell for the whole app (top bar with the elevator dial and breadcrumb,
   bottom deck, two drawers), the stops mapped to places as above, the levels view inside it.
2. **The Mind Chamber**: foam, the gold threshold, spheres by expression and kind, thoughts that
   stay, paths; the visual grammar's three gaps closed.
3. **The drawers wired**: settings to the chamber (Φ, neurotype), telemetry from it (expression,
   mechanism, heart); the pulse down to the heart.
4. **More places**: the remaining atriums and tubes, by the hubs registry (ISS-103).

Left out (ROOTED or later): the EEG bands as a model quantity (a gap in the papers, ISS-101),
a threshold slider (the model fixes it), damping as a setting (not yet a model term).

## The palace and the manifold (author, 15 Oct: keep both)

The author once chose the 11D manifold over a palace ("why a palace? I picked an 11d manifold",
May/June 2026); now both stay, each in its kind:
- **The palace is a place**: a building the mind makes for memory (the method of loci), its 21
  floors the elevator's floors seen from inside the mind, one level up, a map and never the
  territory. Reached from the great hall; you ride its floors and PLACE thoughts in its halls to
  find them again. Later: a thought that stays in the Mind Chamber can be carried to a hall.
- **The manifold is a view**: what every place is underneath, the field's geometry. In the 11D
  view, with Φ high, walls give way and fold (the palace, the atriums, the chamber); at 4D and
  8D they stand. So the palace is what the mind builds, the manifold what it is made of, and the
  same button (11D) shows the one beneath the other.

## Decided (author, 15 Oct)

- The palace and the manifold both kept, as above.
- The Mind Chamber behind the great hall, through the limbic gate.
- The spine first (phase 1), then the Mind Chamber (a first prototype exists).

## Corrections made to HAL's draft (checked against the Lean)

- The blend is not a weighted simplex: EmotionOntology.lean is an algebra (blend, dampen, evoke);
  weights appear only in the field over the mechanisms (SomaField.lean).
- The band is the layer (FieldEvent.lean: an emotion at D8, a thought at D9-11), not the window
  of tolerance or the EEG bands.
- The threshold is in UniversalSomaticField.lean, and fixed (√2): shown, not set.
