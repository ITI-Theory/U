HOW IT FITS TOGETHER

The machine is you; this is the cockpit. One big screen and three tablets beneath it, each showing one thing. With one screen, it is the big one. Click a screen to make it big; MINIMISE (or Esc) puts it back. The limbic field Φ is shared: move it on one screen and every screen answers, each in its own place.
