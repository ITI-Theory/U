# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (preview)

- Renamed **[T]-Theory Explorer** (was Sherlock & the Soma Machine): the theory, the Soma Machine and the
  compiler in one package.
- USC, the Universal Semantic Compiler, beside Sherlock: its findings in Problems (tag errors, tags not yet
  triaged, the method's open assumptions), from `bld/sherlock/usc.txt`; **USC: Check** refreshes them in
  seconds, no Lean.
- **USC: Tag the Selection…**: mark lines as an idea, a loose end, a decision or a question where they were
  said (`{{usc:begin ...}}` and `{{usc:end ...}}` lines), the same as Neovim's `:UscIdea`.
- **USC: Open the Ideas List**.
- **[T]-Theory: Open a Paper or Book…**: the programme's papers, datasets and books (from its registry),
  each opening its DOI; one not yet on Zenodo opens its local PDF when there is one.
- The `.aff` language (affidavits, YAML with dispositions highlighted), and the `{{usc:...}}` tags
  highlighted in Markdown and affidavits.

## 0.1.0 (preview)

- The Soma Machine in an editor tab: fourteen stops, from the whole person to the synapse and
  out to the cosmos, in 3D; Go to a Stop.
- View in Soma Machine: a Lean file opens its tower in the city of code; a document opens the
  stop its text is about.
- Sherlock's findings as errors and warnings in Problems, reloaded when the ledger changes;
  Sherlock: Check; a quick fix to view a finding's declaration.
- Sherlock: Set up: the local build Sherlock's Lean checks need, after a warning.
